Selection rules (worth re-reading before shooting or sourcing):

  • Warm neutrals only — cream, bone, slate, oat. No saturation.
  • Natural light only. Studio strobe reads as commercial.
  • Negative space matters more than the subject.
  • No people, or hands only.
  • No cats staring at the camera. Profile, soft focus, asleep,
    or partly out of frame.
